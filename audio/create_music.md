To answer your questions: **Yes, it is absolutely possible to do this for free using existing AI tools and software.** You do not need to build your own AI from scratch.

Here is the breakdown of how to achieve your "One Tune to Full Orchestra" goal with free tools:

### 1. Is Chordify Free?

* **The Reality:** Chordify has a "Freemium" model. You can search for songs and see chords for free, but features like **uploading your own files** or exporting MIDI are usually locked behind a premium subscription.
* **The Alternative:** Use **[OpenMusic.ai](https://www.openmusic.ai/)** or **[MusicCreator.ai](https://www.musiccreator.ai/)**. These are free web-based AI tools where you upload your audio and they give you a MIDI file for free.

### 2. Can Audacity do this?

* **For Recording:** Yes, Audacity is perfect for recording your initial tune.
* **For Audio-to-MIDI:** Not natively. Audacity is an audio editor, not a MIDI sequencer. While there are experimental plugins (like *Vamp* analyzers), they are very difficult to use for beginners.
* **Verdict:** Use Audacity to record, then use a web AI (like **Basic Pitch**) to convert that recording to MIDI.

### 3. Is Ableton Free? (And is it "Heavy"?)

* **Is it Free?** No. Ableton Live is expensive. However, they offer a **30-day free trial** of the "Suite" (full version). There is also "Ableton Lite," which often comes free with MIDI keyboards, but it's not permanently free for everyone.
* **Is it Heavy?** Yes. It requires at least 8GB of RAM and a decent processor. It can be intimidating for a first-time user.
* **Better Free Alternative:** **[BandLab](https://www.bandlab.com/)** (Web/Mobile). It is 100% free, runs in your browser (not "heavy"), and has professional-sounding instruments built-in.

---

### 4. Step-by-Step "Free AI" Workflow

You can create your orchestra using this chain of free tools:

**Step A: The Tune to MIDI (AI Step)**

1. Record your tune (voice, whistling, or an instrument) on your phone or Audacity.
2. Go to **[Basic Pitch](https://basicpitch.spotify.com/)** (by Spotify). It is a free AI tool.
3. Upload your recording. It will show you the notes.
4. Download the **.mid (MIDI) file**.

**Step B: The "Taal" & Instruments (Arrangement Step)**

1. Open **[BandLab.com](https://www.bandlab.com/)** in your browser.
2. Import your MIDI file.
3. **To change instruments:** Create new tracks. Drag your MIDI notes onto a "Piano" track, a "Flute" track, or a "Violin" track. BandLab has hundreds of free sounds.
4. **To find the Taal:** BandLab has an "Auto-Pitch" and "Tap Tempo" feature to find your speed. For the "Taal," you can search their **Loop Library** for "Tabla" or "Indian Percussion." They have pre-recorded loops in various taals (Teental, Dadra, etc.) that will automatically stretch to match your tune's speed.

### 5. Specialized Indian Instruments (VSTs)

If you want to use a laptop-based software (like the free **Cakewalk** or **GarageBand**) instead of a website, you can download these specific free "Virtual Instruments" (VSTs):

* **Harmonium/Tabla:** **[Samsara Cycle Audio](https://midination.com/vst/free-indian-instrument-vst-plugins/)** offers a free "Sama Harmonium" and a "Tabla" VST.
* **Indian Strings/Flute:** **DSK Indian DreamZ** is a famous free plugin that includes Sitar, Sarod, and Flute.

### Summary: Do you need to build your own AI?

**No.** Building a music AI is a massive engineering task.

* Use **Basic Pitch** for the AI conversion.
* Use **BandLab** or **Cakewalk** for the "Orchestra" generation.
* Use **Samsara/DSK plugins** for the specific Indian sounds.

This setup is **100% free** and requires no coding. You just need to learn how to "copy and paste" MIDI notes from one track to another to build your orchestra!
